3. Linguistic Citation Standards

Adjectives

Masculine Nominative Singular Strong Form (e.g. eall, micel, gōd, wēste).

Verbs

Canonical Infinitive ending in -an, -ian, -on (e.g. secgan, faran, licgan, dōn).

Nouns

Canonical Nominative Singular (e.g. dæġ, stōw, mann, hunta).

Numerals

Masculine Nominative for numbers > 1 (e.g. ān, twēgen for 2, þrīe for 3, fēower).
