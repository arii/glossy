Welcome to the **Glossy Philology & Digital Humanities Journal**.

This journal is dedicated to scholarly essays, editorial guidelines, and research updates on historical linguistics, Old English manuscripts, and interlinear glossing methodology.

> "Philology is that venerable art which demands of its admirers one thing above all: to draw on one side, to take time, to become still, to become slow..."
> — Friedrich Nietzsche, *Morgenröthe* (*Daybreak*), Preface §5
